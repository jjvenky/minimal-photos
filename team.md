# Team

## Terah Sportel

### Principal Investigator

Terah Sportel is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography and Environmental Studies, Wilfrid Laurier University, Canada. She is a development geographer with 10+ years of experience conducting research and outreach in Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Her current research aims to contribute to rural development by focusing on agrarian change, labour dynamics, and sustainable livelihoods.

## Jithin Raj R

### Research Associate

Jithin Raj R is an Independent Researcher in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala,India, specialising in human geography and development studies. He has conducted research on sustainable rural livelihoods, globalisation, migration, agrarian change, and cottage industries (2007 – 2009, 2024  – present). He has extensive experience ethically designing, planning, managing and conducting field research, and with language translations, and spreadsheet design and data entry (n > 569 households; > 2500 individuals).
